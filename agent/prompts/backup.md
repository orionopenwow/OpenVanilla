# Back up my characters and settings

A one-click style backup of your addons, settings and realm data.

Works on: Your copy and your own servers.

## Prompt

```text
Read CLAUDE.md. Make a backup of my Open Vanilla stuff into Backups\manual-<today's date>\: AddOns\, Settings\ (leave out any saved password), Game\benilla-config\ and the config files of my realms in Realms\. For realm characters, tell me how to use the launcher's Backup button or export the character database safely. Then write a short Backups\HOW-TO-RESTORE.txt.
```

## Tips

- Large realm databases can take a while; keep the launcher's servers stopped while you copy files.
