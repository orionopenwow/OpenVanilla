# Make a UI theme

Restyle fonts, colours and frame positions with a small addon of your own.

Works on: Your copy (a new addon in AddOns\Enabled).

## Prompt

```text
Read CLAUDE.md. Make me a small addon called MyTheme in AddOns\Enabled that restyles my interface: <describe the look you want, e.g. darker chat background, bigger font on the player and target frames, gold borders>. Keep everything in one addon I can switch off from the launcher, keep it Lua 5.0 safe, and add /mytheme to turn it on and off. Tell me how to test it with /reload.
```

## Tips

- Describe the look in your own words; screenshots in Screenshots\ help the agent see what you mean.
