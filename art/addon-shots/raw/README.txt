Website pictures of the 11 Classic+ addons. One tight crop per addon window, saved here as <Name>.png.

1. In game, type  /wnl demo  (every window fills with demo data; type it again to stop).
2. Open each window, press Win+Shift+S, drag a box tightly around just that window, and save it here
   with exactly this name:

   Vault.png      /vault      (open your bags)
   Chronicle.png  /chronicle  (the damage meter)
   Compass.png    /compass    (the minimap compass with pins)
   Vanguard.png   /vanguard   (the threat meter)
   Castline.png   /castline   (target an enemy; the demo shows a cast)
   Visage.png     /visage     (enemy nameplates with debuffs; stand near a few mobs)
   Ledger.png     /ledger     (hover any item: the tooltip with prices)
   Concord.png    /concord    (the guild recruitment board)
   Rally.png      /rally      (raid markers and pull timer)
   Scrapper.png   /scrap      (talk to a vendor: the sell-junk/repair bar)
   Persona.png    /rp demo, then /rp list and click Ilyndra (her profile; mouse over a player for the tooltip)

3. Double-click tools\Make_Addon_Shots.bat. It frames each one on the Open Vanilla background and writes
   www\assets\addons\<name>.webp for the website.
