# persona_pair: two characters trade Persona lines on the hidden "OWPersona" channel, in the
# Persona.lua wire format ("#P <rev> <status> <name>~<title>", "?P <target> <knownRev>").
# Client [b] needs a second account: HL_USER2 / HL_PASS2 (web or game account, like --user), and
# optionally HL_CHAR2 (default: its first character). Both characters must be the same faction
# unless the realm allows cross-faction channels.
login
[b] login
join OWPersona
[b] join OWPersona
expect joined OWPersona within 10s
[b] expect joined OWPersona within 10s
say-channel OWPersona #P 1 ooc Herald~Chronicler
[b] expect channel OWPersona from ${HL_CHAR:-Herald} matches ^#P 1 ooc Herald~Chronicler$ within 15s
[b] say-channel OWPersona #P 1 ic Second~Scribe
expect channel OWPersona matches ^#P 1 ic Second~Scribe$ within 15s
[b] say-channel OWPersona ?P Herald 0
expect channel OWPersona matches ^\?P Herald 0$ within 15s
leave OWPersona
[b] leave OWPersona
logout
[b] logout
