# transmog_learning: the two other ways looks are collected. [a] (a Warfront level 60 wearing a
# two-handed weapon): an enchantment's glow becomes an illusion, saved over the real enchantment
# and switched with the outfit. [b] (HL_USER2): a quest's reward choices are all learned, the
# ones not taken too ("The Grand Betrayal": cloth, leather and mail legs; [b] takes the mail,
# and the cloth it left is announced as new).

[b] expect wnl HI within 10s

[b] send .wnl hi

send .go xyz -4835.5 -855.2 502 0

send .wnl tm use 1
