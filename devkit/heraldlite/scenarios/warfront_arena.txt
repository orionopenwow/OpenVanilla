# warfront_arena: [a] and [b] queue the 1v1 duel ladder, are taken to the Ring of Law, prepare,
# fight; [b] logging out mid-fight counts as leaving, so [a] wins and is sent home.
# Needs two level 60 Warfront characters on different accounts, out of combat, not grouped.
# It leaves [b] locked out of arena queues for 15 minutes (the leaver rule): run it last.
login
[b] login
expect wnl HI within 10s
[b] expect wnl HI within 10s
send .wnl hi
[b] send .wnl hi
send .wnl wf hi
[b] send .wnl wf hi
send .wnl wf q join d1
[b] send .wnl wf q join d1
expect wnl WQ matches ^pop,d1,\d+,[01]$ within 20s
[b] expect wnl WQ matches ^pop,d1,\d+,[01]$ within 20s
expect wnl WQ matches ^prep,\d+$ within 60s
[b] expect wnl WQ matches ^prep,\d+$ within 60s
expect wnl WQ matches ^go$ within 75s
[b] expect wnl WQ matches ^go$ within 75s
[b] logout
expect wnl WQ matches ^end,1,\d+,\d+$ within 15s
wait 12s
