# warfront_streak: any death ends a kill streak. A streak of 7 (set with the game-master test hook)
# ended by dying to something that isn't a player pays no bounty and tells the zone.
# Needs: a level 60 Warfront character on a game-master account, in the open world (not an instance).
login
expect wnl HI within 10s
send .wnl wf hi
expect wnl WF matches ^1, within 10s
send .wnl wf streak 7
expect wnl WD matches ^streak,7$ within 10s
target self
send .die
expect wnl WN matches ^ended,[^,]+,7$ within 10s
send .revive
wait 2s
send .wnl wf streak 0
expect wnl WD matches ^streak,0$ within 10s
send .die
wait 3s
send .revive
logout
