## What Open Vanilla is {#what}

Open Vanilla is a free, community-run home for classic Vanilla. You can play on the official **Vanilla Remastered** realm (Vanilla with careful quality-of-life additions), play **solo offline** with bots, or **host your own realm** for friends. It's built on the open-source **benilla** client and runs in your browser or on Windows.

> **Note:** Open Vanilla is a fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard, it sells nothing, and it never ships Blizzard's game files: you use a copy of Vanilla Client v1.3 you obtain yourself.

## Three ways to play {#ways}

| Way | Best for | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Browser** | Trying it right now, phones and tablets, any PC | A browser with WebGPU; nothing to install |
| **Launcher** (Windows) | The full experience, your own addons, Solo and hosting | Windows 10/11 64-bit and Vanilla Client v1.3 (the launcher can fetch it) |
| **Play Solo** | Offline play with bots, no other players | The launcher, a few GB of disk, 8 GB+ RAM recommended |

## Quick start: browser {#quick-browser}

1. Open [play.openvanilla.app](https://play.openvanilla.app/).
2. Press **Sign in with Discord**, or **Play as guest** to try it first.
3. Create a character and play. The first load downloads game data; after that it loads from your disk.

Details: [Playing in the browser](browser.html).

## Quick start: launcher {#quick-launcher}

1. Download the launcher from the [home page](../index.html) (**Desktop Client**): `OpenVanilla-Setup-<version>.exe` is the recommended installer, or the zip for a portable copy.
2. Start it. On **Home**, the bar above **PLAY** says what's missing; pressing PLAY fixes it: it downloads Vanilla Client v1.3 or lets you pick the folder you already have.
3. Press **PLAY**. The first time, it signs you in with Discord.

Details: [The launcher](launcher.html).

> **Note:** The launcher isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Unknown publisher" or show SmartScreen. Choose **More info → Run anyway**, or check the file against the checksums on the release page first.

## Your account {#account}

- **Sign in with Discord** is the one button for new and returning players: the first time, it creates your realm account and adds you to the Open Vanilla Discord. Open Vanilla never sees your Discord password or messages.
- **Guests** get a real account tied to that device (kept for 30 days of inactivity). Press **Save your heroes — link Discord** in game to keep your characters for good.
- Accounts live on each realm: a community realm has its own accounts and its own website.
- Staff will never ask for your password.

## What you need: Vanilla Client v1.3 {#client}

The desktop game needs your own copy of **Vanilla Client v1.3** (English, build 5875). The launcher's game setup page either downloads one (**Download & install**, a few GB, resumable) or checks a folder you already have (**Select existing folder**). It never changes the game's files. More on [clients.html](../clients.html) and in [The launcher](launcher.html#client-setup).
