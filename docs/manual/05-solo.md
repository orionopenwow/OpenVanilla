## What it is {#what}

**Play Solo** runs a complete Vanilla realm on your own PC: the SPP Classics server with **playerbots**, computer-controlled characters who join your group for dungeons and group quests and roam the world. It listens only on your own PC and works offline once it's set up.

| Good to know | |
| --- | --- |
| Needs | Windows 10/11 64-bit, your own Vanilla Client v1.3 (English, build 5875), a few GB of disk (SSD helps), 8 GB+ RAM recommended |
| Internet | Only for the first download and updates |
| Characters | Stay on your PC: they can't move to or from online realms |
| Players | None: no trading, guilds or chat with people |
| Vanilla Remastered | The client-side Vanilla Remastered addons work; the official realm's server changes (AoE loot and more) aren't there |

## Set it up {#setup}

1. In the launcher, switch Home to **SOLO** and press **PLAY**.
2. It downloads the solo server (about 110 MB, then about 700 MB of server files). Both resume if interrupted.
3. The server's setup window opens once: type **1**, wait for the Vanilla menu, then type **0**.
4. PLAY turns ready by itself: **READY · SOLO ON THIS PC**.

Already have SPP Classics? Choose **I have SPP Classics…** and point the launcher at it.

## Play {#play}

Press **PLAY** in Solo mode. The launcher starts the database, login and world servers hidden in the background and logs you in as **SOLO / SOLO**. Closing the game saves the world and stops the servers.

## Bots {#bots}

Playerbots fill the world and will group with you. Ask them to follow, attack or assist in party chat, or let **Herald** play your own character (`/herald`, see [In-game features](classic-plus.html#helpers)): Play Solo has Herald's self-bot switched on.

## Change your solo realm {#change}

It's your realm: you can change XP rates, add NPCs and give yourself GM powers. The config lives under `Realms\Solo\...\mangosd.conf`. The easiest way is to ask an AI agent with the starter prompts in [Connect an AI](ai.html#prompts), or by hand in [Edit your own game](editing.html#realm-rules). Back up first.
