**Warfront** is a separate PvP realm where everyone is level 60 and ready to fight. Its window appears only on Warfront; on other realms it stays hidden. Until it opens, the website shows it as coming soon.

## Your first login: pick a kit {#kits}

On your first login a window welcomes you and asks you to **pick a kit**: a level-60 class and spec loadout for your faction with pre-enchanted gear (for example, an Arms Warrior in Field Marshal's plate). Open the window again any time with `/wf` (or `/warfront`, `/kit`).

## Gear tiers and the shop {#gear}

- **Gear picker** (`/wf gear`): three tiers, **Starter**, **Tier 2** and **Tier 3**. Higher tiers unlock with arena rating or honorable kills.
- **Shop** (`/wf shop`): consumables for gold.
- **Shared sets** (`/wf sets`): save up to 10 sets and share them; others can vote, and three reports hide a set.

Kits, gear and the shop can't be used in combat, while dead, inside an instance or while queued.

## Arenas and the duel ladder {#arena}

Open the arena tab with `/arena` (or `/wf arena`). Level 60 only; the party leader queues the party.

| Command | Queue |
| --- | --- |
| `/arena s2` | 2v2 Skirmish |
| `/arena s3` | 3v3 Skirmish |
| `/arena r2` | 2v2 Rated |
| `/arena r3` | 3v3 Rated |
| `/arena d1` | 1v1 Duel Ladder |
| `/arena leave` | Leave the queue |

When the queue pops you get a 60-second preparation countdown, then **Fight!** Live queue counts (battlegrounds too) refresh every 10 seconds.

## Kill streaks and bounties {#bounties}

Long kill streaks are announced in chat and put a bounty on the player; take them down and you'll see **Bounty collected**. During bot-fill hours the realm fills battlegrounds with bots; you're told when you enter, and bots never count for streaks or bounties.
