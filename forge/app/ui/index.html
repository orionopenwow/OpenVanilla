    Setup

      Forge only reads your own copy of the game, to show you its files and to make sure a pack never ships one of them.

      What Forge does

      • World: sculpt, paint and flood the ground; place props, buildings, creatures and portals; scatter, roads and prefabs; make whole new maps. See it with your game's own textures and models, in 2D or 3D, with undo.
      • Models: your own .glb becomes a creature, mount or prop, with real collision.
      • Content: creatures, quests, items and vendors from short forms.
      • Behaviour: fight plans, lines to say, dialogue and patrol paths for your creatures, no tables.
      • Data: every realm and client table as rows, with the SQL and DBC changes built for you.
      • Code: write addons with a Lua 5.0 lint, and run them in a fake client.
      • Test in your own client and Play Solo realm; with Live Link, apply changes while you play and jump to any spot. Then take it all back out.
      • Publish: a signed pack a realm can review and load.
      • Assets: browse and preview every file in your game.

                                      Try it in the game
                                      
          
                                      Forge tests on this PC only, on your own test server: its own copies of your game and your Play Solo realm. Your game folder and your own Play Solo are never changed, and testing never connects to Open Vanilla's realms or anyone else's. Setup guide
                                      
          
                                      An older Forge left test files in your real game or Play Solo.Forge can take out exactly what it put there (nothing else is touched).
                                      
          
                                        
          
                                          
            
                                        1. Build and installPuts the pack in the test copy of your game and queues its rows for the test realm. Again after each change.
                                          2. 
            
                                        2. Start the test realmForge's copy of Play Solo, with the pack's rows applied as it starts, and the test copy of your game.
                                          3. 
            
                                        3. Sign in and lookAccount solo. Press Make me a GM, then use the commands on the right to find what you made.
                                          4. 
          
                                        
          
                                        Or skip all three: Test in game (T) on the World screen builds, starts and puts you on the spot you're looking at.
                                        
          
                                        
          
                                          
          
                                          Open Vanilla folder
            
                                          
            
                                          Where Play Solo lives (the folder with Play Solo.bat). Found automatically when your game folder is it.
                                          
          
                                          How it works
          
                                            
            
                                          • The test copy of your game sits beside it (<your game> (Forge test)). The big archives are shared with your game (hard links: no extra space, and the game only reads them); settings and addons are copies, so what the test changes stays there.
                                            • 
            
                                          • It gets Data\patch-~.MPQ: the pack's files, plus the client tables with your rows merged in, loading after Open Vanilla's own patches.
                                            • 
            
                                          • The test realm is a copy of your Play Solo made the first time (stop Play Solo for that). Its database is its own from then on, so your solo characters come along and nothing done in testing reaches your own realm.
                                            • 
            
                                          • Remove takes the test build out of the copies; the test realm undoes your rows when it next starts.
                                            • 
            
                                          • Only this PC. The test copy of your game signs in to this PC (127.0.0.1), even when started by hand, and the test realm listens to this PC only.
                                            • 
            
                                          • With friends: host your work on your own server. How

                                          Publish 
                                          
          
                                            
          
                                            
          
                                            
        
                                            
        
                                            
          
                                            What reviewers check
                                            
          
                                              
            
                                            • The signature: nothing changed after you signed it, and it's your key.
                                              • 
            
                                            • Only your own work: no file of the game's, no models converted from the game's own files, a credit for every model and every asset you didn't make.
                                              • 
            
                                            • The SQL is only what Forge writes, on world tables, inside your ID ranges. Never accounts or characters.
                                              • 
            
                                            • Content packs are installed by realms, after a staging test. Addons can be installed by players.
                                              • 
          
                                            
          
                                            Your creator key
                                            
          
                                            The Workshop ties this key to your account. Keep Forge's config folder backed up: a new key means a new creator.
                                            
          
          
                                            Sending it
                                            
          
                                            Uploads from Forge come with the Workshop's pack queue. Until then, send the .ovpack through Submit your mod on the Mods tab of openvanilla.app.
