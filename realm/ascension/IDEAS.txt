1. Core Engine & Client Architecture (What You Need to Build It)To support a classless or multi-archetype environment, the base client and server core require four foundational engineering layers:A. Unified Class Masking & Spellbook ExpansionSingle Base Archetype ("The Adventurer"): On the server core, all characters roll under a single unified class mask (or unlocked bitmask across all existing classes) so they can equip any weapon/armor type and learn any spell ID without the engine rejecting cross-class abilities.Multi-Tab UI Injection: Standard MMO clients cap the number of spellbook tabs and talent trees. You need custom client UI panels (via Lua/XML or WebGPU/Canvas overlays) that dynamically render 9 to 20+ scrollable talent trees and a searchable Master Spell Catalog.Dynamic Resource Bars: A character using melee energy skills alongside mana spells and rage finishers needs either a Unified Resource Conversion system or Multi-Bar HUD support that displays Mana, Rage, Energy, and Combo Points simultaneously when relevant abilities are learned.B. The Dual-Currency Point EconomyTo prevent every player from picking the exact same 10 overpowered spells, the server tracks two currencies gained per level:Ability Points (AP): Used to unlock active spells and passive skills. High-impact utilities (e.g., Immunities, Teleports, Stealth, Dual-Wielding Two-Handers) carry a heavy AP cost (3–4 points), while core rotational fillers cost 1–2 AP.Talent Points (TP): Spent across any talent tree without prerequisite class locks, allowing hybrid passive scaling.C. Custom Spell-Scripting & Proc EngineA server-side hook system (SpellScripts / AuraScripts) capable of intercepting combat events (OnHit, OnCrit, OnCast, OnKill) and dynamically modifying spell behavior based on equipped runes/enchants.PvP vs. PvE Stat Split: Separate damage, healing, and crowd-control duration multipliers for player-versus-player targets so broken PvE combos don't result in 1-shot PvP metas.2. The Complete Feature SetI. Three Distinct Gameplay ModesFree-Pick Architect Mode: Players have total freedom to spend their Ability and Talent Points at any time out of combat to theorycraft exact builds from a master catalog.Wildcard / Draft Mode (Roguelike Leveling):At Level 1, an on-screen 3-Card Draft UI presents three random starter abilities.Every 2 levels, the player draws a new hand of 3 random abilities/talents to add to their kit.At max level, players earn Reroll Tokens / Unlearning Scrolls from dungeons and PvP to gradually swap out weak drafted cards and refine their build.Custom Hero Classes Mode: Uses the exact same backend tools to lock sets of custom spells and custom talent trees into 15–20+ brand-new thematic classes (e.g., Necromancer, Pyromancer, Chronomancer, Ranger, Witch Doctor, Tinker).II. The Build-Defining "Rune / Enchant" Altar SystemThe glue that turns random spells into an actual build is an Account-Wide Enchantment Collection:Tiered Enchant Slots: Every armor/weapon slot holds a custom modifier slot—capped at 1 Legendary, 3 Epic, and 6 Rare modifiers active at once.Skill-Altering Passives: Instead of flat stats, Legendary and Epic modifiers rewrite ability mechanics (e.g., "Your melee critical strikes instantly cast a free Chain Lightning" or "Your Fireball now heals friendly targets and applies a stacking armor buff").Altar Extraction & Rolling: Players disenchant runes found in the world to permanently add them to an account-wide collection book, or spend currency at an Enchanting Altar to roll random modifiers onto their gear.III. The "Build Browser" (In-Game Community Loadouts)Because 1,000+ spells overwhelm new players, an integrated In-Game Build Browser is essential:Players can browse top-rated community builds filtered by role (Tank, Healer, Melee DPS, Ranged Caster, PvP Burst).Clicking "Activate Build Template" highlights the exact spells to pick at each level, auto-allocates talents, and lists the required gear runes.IV. Replayability: Prestige & Leveling TrialsTo keep the open world populated forever, max-level is treated as a loop rather than a dead end:The Prestige Loop: At max level, a player can reset their character to Level 1 while banking their gear and mounts. Completing a Prestige run grants Prestige Currency for cosmetics, bank tabs, and reroll tokens, plus lets Draft-mode players roll a fresh deck of spells.Opt-In Leveling Trials: At Level 1, players can toggle challenge modifiers at an NPC for exclusive titles, auras, and rewards:Hardcore (1-Life): Permadeath or forced drop to normal mode upon dying.Ironman / Solo Self-Found: No trading, mail, auction house, or party grouping.High-Scale World Difficulty: Increases all open-world monster health and damage by +100% to +300% and gives basic mobs elite mechanics in exchange for higher drop rates.V. Multi-Pillar Endgame (Solo, Small-Group, Raid, & PvP)Players can earn top-tier gear through four completely independent tracks:Endgame PillarCore Mechanics & Progression1. High-Risk Open World PvPPlayers can toggle High-Risk Mode in the open world. High-Risk zones spawn exclusive crafting nodes and world bosses, but dying to another player drops a portion of your inventory or equipped items into a lootable chest.2. Endless Roguelike TowerA 1-to-5 player instanced dungeon where floors scale infinitely. After every floor boss, players pick temporary roguelike buffs (+50% Haste, exploding enemies, extra projectiles) to push higher leaderboards.3. Mythic+ & Multi-Tier RaidsClassic 5-man dungeons scaled with timed keystones and rotating affixes. Classic raids scaled dynamically for 10–25 players across 4 difficulty tiers (Normal, Heroic, Mythic, Ascended).4. Daily Bounty CallboardsMajor cities feature a Bounty Board with solo open-world objectives and battleground tasks that reward Loot Caches capable of dropping raid-equivalent gear with random ARPG bonus affixes (+Leech, +Speed, +Cleave).
