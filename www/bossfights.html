How it works

1. Fight now

Play as a guest or sign in. Your character lands in Gurubashi Arena at level 60, with gear sets, talents and full bags.

2. Queue

Pick a raid, a tier and your role. Alone, as a party, or ten friends at once.

3. The raid pops

Your group gets its own copy of the raid. If nobody else comes in 30 seconds, bots take the empty seats.

4. Straight to the boss

In Onyxia's Lair you start in her room, with no trash on the way. The clock starts at the first pull.

5. Clear it

The next tier opens, your best clear gives a small stat aura in Boss Fights, and the run goes on the boards.

Tiers

Top line: boss health and damage against the stock raid. Bottom: the stat bonus your best clear gives (all stats, Boss Fights only, at most +10%). Tier affixes: Fortified (trash +25%), Tyrannical (bosses +25%), Enrage (bosses hit harder the longer a fight lasts).

Weekly twists

One extra rule each week, on top of the tier affixes. Herald posts it in Discord on Monday.

Your group, your raid

Every group gets its own copy of the raid. No one else walks in.

Nothing for sale

Gear, titles and bonuses are earned in Boss Fights and stay there.

Fair and open

The same rules for everyone. Raids with bots rank on their own board, and every clear came from the realm itself.
