How tiers work

1. Tier 1 is the raid as you know it

Stock bosses, scaled to a 10-player group.

2. Each tier gets harder

Bosses and trash gain health and hit harder, and each tier adds a twist.

3. Clear it to unlock the next

A clear opens the next tier of that raid, and your best clear anywhere makes your characters stronger in Boss Fights.

Top line: boss and trash health and damage against the stock raid. Bottom: the stat bonus your best clear gives (all stats, Boss Fights only, at most +10%). Twists: Fortified (trash +25%), Tyrannical (bosses +25%), Enrage (bosses hit harder the longer a fight lasts).

Your group, your raid
