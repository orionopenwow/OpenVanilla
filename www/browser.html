Everything in your browser

Open Vanilla runs in a browser tab. Play, add addons from the Workshop, keep an eye on your Vanilla Agents and start a content pack, all without installing anything. The desktop app is still there for the few things a browser can't do, listed at the bottom.

Still in the desktop app

  • Play Solo: your own world on your PC, offline. It runs a server, so it can't live in a tab.
  • Vanilla Forge, in full: models, art, maps, the 3D tools, Test & Play and Publish. Get Vanilla Forge.
  • Workshop addons that use XML frames (pfUI, Bagshui and others): the desktop app installs them in one click.
