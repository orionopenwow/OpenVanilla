The Realm Covenant & Continuity Pledge

Open Vanilla is not one destination realm. It is a federated standard where thousands of independent realms exist, players hold their signed character records forever, and communities survive any single shutdown.

🛡️ 1. Non-Commercial (Nothing Sold)
No cash shops, pay-to-win items, or donation perks. Voluntary contributions may only cover verified server hosting costs.
📦 2. Bring Your Own Game Data
Realms never host or distribute Blizzard game files. Players bring their own client data. Community packs contain only original or open-licenced assets.
🤖 3. Transparent Bots Policy
Playerbots must always be clearly labelled (e.g. RNDBOT_) and reported strictly apart from human players in heartbeats. Faking population is strictly banned.
📜 4. 30–90 Day Closure Notice & Character Export
Hosts agree to give 30 to 90 days notice before shutting down and keep signed character Passport export active so no player's journey is lost.
🤝 5. The Realm Will & Emergency Second Admin
Every listed realm names an emergency successor and maintains encrypted backup escrow. Custom packs are released under an open licence upon closure.
