1 · Arrive ready

Sign in with Google or as a guest and make a level 60 of any class. You land in Gurubashi Arena with your class's abilities on your bars, potions in your bags and a full set of gear on. The Armory (the tab on the left, or the minimap button) puts on any gear piece by piece and saves your loadouts.

2 · Queue, accept, fight

Press Duel now (rated), Practice duel, 2v2 or 3v3. You're pooled with everyone else in that queue; when a match is found you both get an accept window, then you go to your own copy of the arena. Best of three rounds, sudden death if it drags on.

3 · Climb, bet, host

Rated wins move you up the ladder below. Watch any fight from the stands and bet Duel Gold on it. Send a friend your invite link to party up, or host your own server with your rules and its own ladder.
