    New hereStart playing in five minutes, in your browser or with the launcher. In the gameEvery Vanilla Remastered feature and command, and how to switch them off. Use your AIConnect Claude or another AI agent to make addons and change your realm. Make it yoursAddons, UI, realm rules and models, tested before you share them. PublishPut an addon, a fork or a repo on the Workshop. Host a realmRun your own server for friends, on your PC or a VPS.
