Realms

Every Open Vanilla realm that is up right now, in three tiers: Verified realms (Open Vanilla's own, and community realms the maintainer has vetted), Community P2P realms (hosted by players, checked automatically, not vetted), and the Verified P2P nodes that carry them. To play, open a realm in the Open Vanilla launcher (Realms tab) and press Join.

Verified P2P nodes

Nodes are volunteers' relays: players connect through them, so hosts open no ports and nobody's address is shown. They carry only end-to-end encrypted game traffic. Supporters & how to become one
Play without Open Vanilla. If this list is down, Open Vanilla is offline: you can still host and join friends directly. In the launcher, host with “Direct from my home connection” or “My local network only” and send a guest invite; friends paste it in the Realms tab or use Join by address. In direct mode your friends see your home IP address, so only invite people you trust.

Private realms aren't listed; they're joined with an invite link from their host. Ping isn't shown here: the launcher measures it from your PC.
