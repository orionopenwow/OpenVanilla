Community rules

Version 1 · 20 October 2026. One set of rules for the game, World and guild chat, the website and the Discord. Your Discord and your game account are one account, so they're one community.

The short version. Be decent. Keep it all-ages. Play fair. Nothing is for sale. Report problems instead of fighting them. People, not bots, decide every action.

1. Be decent

  • No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats or personal attacks, in game or on Discord.
  • No attacks on anyone for their race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, sexuality, religion, disability or age.
  • Banter is fine when everyone's in on it. When someone asks you to stop, stop.
  • Don't follow, grief or camp a player who has asked you to leave them alone.

2. Keep it all-ages

  • Players are 13 and up. No sexual content, gore or shock content in chat, names, guild names, posts or images.
  • No romantic or sexual approaches to anyone who is, or seems to be, under 18.
  • Adults don't ask young players to move to private chats, other apps, or to keep secrets.

3. Protect privacy

  • No doxxing: never share someone's real name, address, photos, social accounts or IP address without their permission.
  • Don't ask other players for personal information.
  • Got a private message that breaks these rules? Send it to staff rather than posting it to shame someone.

4. Play fair

  • No cheats, hacks, third-party bots or automation, and no exploiting bugs on purpose.
  • Herald's autopilot is allowed. It's part of the realm, so it's the one bot that's fine to use.
  • Found a bug? Report it with the blue bug button by the chat. Don't use it, and don't spread it.
  • One person per account. Don't share your account or your password.
  • Don't attack, flood or probe the servers.

5. Nothing is for sale

  • No buying or selling gold, items, characters, accounts or boosts for real money or anything outside the game.
  • Open Vanilla sells nothing. Anyone offering to sell you something "from Open Vanilla" is scamming you.

6. Chat and channels

World chat

  • World is shared by the whole realm and bridged to the Discord's #world. What you say in either place is seen in both.
  • Keep it about the game and keep it friendly. No flooding, repeated lines, ALL CAPS walls or arguments about politics or religion.
  • Links are blocked. Trading goes in Trade and #trade; groups go in LookingForGroup and #looking-for-group.

On Discord

  • Use the right channel. Bugs go in #bug-reports, one post per bug. Questions go in #help.
  • No ads, server invites or unrequested self-promotion. Streams and creations have their own channels.
  • Don't mass-mention people or ping staff for things that aren't urgent.

7. Names

  • Character, guild and pet names can't be hateful, sexual or rude, mock a real person, or pretend to be staff.
  • The realm refuses the worst words outright. Staff can ask anyone else to rename.

8. Scams and impersonation

  • Staff will never ask for your password. Not in game, not on Discord, not anywhere.
  • Herald never messages you first. A "Herald" or "staff" DM asking for anything is a scam: report it.
  • No fake giveaways, phishing links or pretending to be staff. In game, real staff have a blue <GM> tag by their name.

9. How to report

  • In game: right-click the player's name, then Report. The report includes the chat around it, so you don't need screenshots.
  • On Discord: use Discord's own Report option on the message, and ping a Moderator if it's urgent.
  • Don't fight back or start a pile-on. Report, ignore, and let staff handle it.
  • If someone is in danger (threats of violence or self-harm), contact your local emergency services first, then tell staff.

10. What happens when rules are broken

  • Staff choose what fits. Usually it's a reminder or a warning, then a mute (10 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours), then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban.
  • Serious harm skips the line: threats, sexual content involving minors, doxxing, scams or attacks on the servers can mean an immediate permanent ban. Where the law or Discord's rules call for it, we report it.
  • A ban covers the Discord and the game together, because they're one account.
  • Herald may flag or briefly mute obvious spam, but people decide every lasting action.

11. Appeals

  • Think a decision was wrong? Open a post in #help and explain calmly. Another staff member will look at it.
  • One appeal per decision. Arguing in public channels or DMing every moderator doesn't speed it up.

12. Staff follow the rules too

  • Staff don't give out items, gold or favours, and they don't play favourites.
  • Staff powers are only for running the realm. If a staff member breaks these rules, report it like anything else: it goes to the rest of the team.

These rules sit alongside the Terms of use and the Privacy page. Questions: #help on the Open Vanilla Discord.
