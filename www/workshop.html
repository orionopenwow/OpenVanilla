Share an addon

Made a Vanilla addon, or fixed one up for Vanilla Remastered? Upload it as a zip (10 MB at most). It waits in a review queue: we load it in the Vanilla client simulator, look for anything unsafe (chat sent on its own, other addons' saved data, os/io), and a moderator checks it and its licence before it is listed. We only host addons whose licence allows it (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, GPL, LGPL, MPL-2.0, Unlicense, CC0); for anything else, send us the author's link instead.

Making one? Vanilla Forge writes and checks addons for the game client, and runs them in a pretend client before you start the game.

The badges come from an automatic check: the addon is loaded in a simulated Vanilla client, through login and a few seconds of play. Works loaded clean; Partly loaded but uses parts of the client the simulator does not model (judge it in the game); Needs fixes showed errors; addons we may not host are not checked. Ratings, reviews and install counts are live from the realm; when it cannot be reached, the page shows the last snapshot and is read-only.
